# ava-universe
🌌 Browser-based deep space signal analyzer — scan the hydrogen line, collect anomalies, reply to aliens &amp; chat with an AI astronomer. Free. Open source.
AvA Universe is a citizen science platform for radio signal analysis. Scan the 1420 MHz hydrogen line, collect candidate signals, encode replies to alien transmissions, explore live Moon data, and chat with Dr. Elara Voss — an AI-powered astronomer. All in your browser. No server. No cost.
